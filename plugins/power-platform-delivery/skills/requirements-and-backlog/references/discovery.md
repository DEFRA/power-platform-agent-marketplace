# Discovery

Use this to understand a new or unclear need before writing stories. Ask only the questions that matter for the need in front of you, a few at a time.

## Questions by topic

| Topic                | Find out                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Objective            | The problem, who has it, why it matters now and how success will be measured                             |
| People               | Who uses the outcome, who's affected, who decides and who approves                                       |
| Current process      | How it works today, where it fails and what workarounds people use                                       |
| Scope                | What's in, what's out and what's deferred                                                                |
| Rules and data       | The business rules, calculations and validations, and the data used, created or changed, with its source |
| Dependencies         | Other teams, systems, integrations, data migrations and decisions the work relies on                     |
| Constraints          | Deadlines, policy, legislation, licensing, budget and technology limits                                  |
| Non-functional needs | Use the prompts below                                                                                    |

## Challenge the solution

A request often names a solution, such as "add a button that…" or "build a flow to…". Ask what problem it solves and whether something simpler would do. Record the underlying need as the requirement and the suggested solution as one option.

## Non-functional prompts

Ask about each of these. If the user can't answer one, record it as an open question rather than leave it out.

| Need           | Ask                                                                                                |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Security       | Who may see, create, change or delete the data? Is any of it sensitive or personal?                |
| Performance    | How quickly must it respond, and under what load?                                                  |
| Scalability    | How many users and records are there now, and how many in a few years?                             |
| Availability   | When must it be available, and what happens if it isn't?                                           |
| Accessibility  | Who are the users, what assistive technology do they use and which accessibility standard applies? |
| Compliance     | Which legislation, policy or retention rules apply?                                                |
| Auditability   | What must be recorded about who did what, and when?                                                |
| Supportability | Who supports it, and what do they need to diagnose a problem?                                      |

## Label every fact

| Label     | Meaning                                                               |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Confirmed | Stated by someone who knows, or found in a source the user pointed to |
| Assumed   | Your inference. Say who should confirm it.                            |
| Unknown   | Needed but not answered yet. Record it as an open question.           |

## Output

Use these headings, in this order:

1. Business objective
2. Users and stakeholders
3. Current process
4. Scope (in, out and later)
5. Business rules
6. Data
7. Dependencies
8. Constraints
9. Non-functional needs
10. Assumptions and risks
11. Open questions

Write "None identified" under any heading with nothing to report.
